Keep ALL original local hues at 60–70% saturation slightly warmed by old yellow-brown parchment texture, never monochrome or sepia-only. Magic/fire/eyes retain original hue and brightest saturation. Paper fibres/grain, faint stains/watermarks show through paint. Ancient legend book, 2.5D motion comic. Preserve depth, clear subject silhouette, softer lighter distant details. No text/logos/watermarks, neon, photorealism, 3D, anime, cartoons, cel shading, glossy surfaces or digital airbrushing. 

Variantes por plano:
fondo: Opaque BACKGROUND animation layer. Preserve full canvas and exact scene alignment. Aged parchment across whole image, rough burnt vignette at four corners, small ink splatters/drips only near edges never faces. Distant background lighter, softer, atmospheric haze. Do not add scene objects.
medio: Transparent MIDGROUND animation layer. Preserve full canvas, object placement and original alpha silhouette. All empty areas stay truly transparent. Parchment-like texture ONLY within existing painted shapes; no paper background, no new border, no added scene. Clear readable silhouette.
frente: Transparent FOREGROUND animation layer. Preserve full canvas, object placement and exact original alpha silhouette. All empty areas stay truly transparent. Parchment-like texture ONLY within existing painted shapes; no paper background, no new border or new scene. Confident detailed dark ink.

Se reforzaron en cada capa sus colores locales, anatomia, objetos y simbolos existentes. Los originales se preservan en originales/. Las vistas son composiciones de revision, no capas adicionales. Dos salidas de 1671 px se completaron con un pixel transparente a la derecha, sin escalar. imagen4_frente conserva los 940 px de altura del original.
La generacion conserva la composicion general, pero no garantiza identidad geometrica pixel por pixel: hay pequenas variaciones en contornos y detalles y un calentamiento visible de la paleta.
